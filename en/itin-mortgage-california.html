Can You Get a Mortgage With an ITIN in California?

Yes, but the options are narrower than with a Social Security number. Fannie Mae requires each borrower to have a valid SSN or ITIN (B2-2-01), and non-U.S. citizens must be lawful permanent or non-permanent residents (B2-2-02), so an ITIN works for a Fannie Mae loan only together with lawful residency. Most other ITIN mortgages are lender-specific non-QM products whose down payment, rate and documentation rules vary; FHA is not an option, because it now requires U.S. citizenship or permanent residence.

What an ITIN is, and what it is not

An Individual Taxpayer Identification Number is a 9-digit number the IRS issues for federal tax purposes to people who need a U.S. taxpayer number but are not eligible for a Social Security number. You apply with Form W-7. The IRS is clear about its limits: an ITIN does not authorize work in the U.S., does not provide or change immigration status, does not qualify you for Social Security benefits or the Earned Income Tax Credit, and is not identification outside the federal tax system.

What the official rules say

  • Fannie Mae: each borrower needs a valid SSN or ITIN. Fannie Mae buys loans to non-U.S. citizens who are lawful permanent or non-permanent residents on the same terms as citizens; the lender decides which documents prove status and, by delivering the loan, warrants that the borrower is legally present. An ITIN alone does not establish lawful residency.
  • Freddie Mac: has its own borrower eligibility rules in Guide Section 5103.2; they are not identical to Fannie Mae's.
  • FHA: since HUD Mortgagee Letter 2025-09, required for case numbers assigned on or after May 25, 2025, FHA borrowers must be U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents (with a narrow category for citizens of Micronesia, the Marshall Islands and Palau).

Keep your ITIN active

Lenders will want to see tax returns filed with your ITIN, and the number has to be valid. According to the IRS, an ITIN that is not used on a U.S. federal tax return for any 3 consecutive tax years expires on December 31 after the third year of non-use. You renew it with Form W-7 by checking the renewal box. If you later receive an SSN, tell your lender before it pulls credit or verifies your tax returns.

Lender-specific ITIN programs

Outside the Fannie Mae path, ITIN mortgages are portfolio or non-QM products. Each lender writes its own rules for credit history (including whether it accepts rent and utility payment records), how many years of ITIN tax returns it wants, down payment, reserves, property types, and whether there is a prepayment penalty. This page does not quote rates or minimum down payments because they are lender-specific and change. Compare written Loan Estimates from more than one lender.

Questions to ask any ITIN lender

  • Is this a conventional (Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac) loan or your own ITIN program?
  • Which documents do you need to verify identity and residency?
  • How many years of tax returns filed with my ITIN do you need, and do you accept non-traditional credit?
  • Is there a prepayment penalty? Can I see the full terms on a Loan Estimate?

Documents to start gathering

  • The IRS letter that assigned your ITIN.
  • Passport and any immigration documents you hold.
  • Federal tax returns filed with your ITIN, plus W-2s, 1099s or business records.
  • Bank statements showing the down payment and where it came from.
  • Records of on-time rent and utility payments, if you have little U.S. credit.

For immigration questions, talk to an immigration attorney. For tax filing questions, talk to a CPA or enrolled agent.

Worked example

This is a made-up scenario to show how the rules apply. It is not a real client.

Lin is in the U.S. on an H-4 visa as the spouse of an H-1B worker. She has no work authorization and no SSN, and she got an ITIN so the couple could file a joint tax return. They want to buy a home together. Under Fannie Mae's rules, Lin can be a borrower with her ITIN because she is lawfully present; her husband's salary would be the qualifying income. FHA is not available to either of them, because neither is a U.S. citizen or permanent resident. If Lin were instead buying on her own with ITIN-reported self-employment income, the couple would be looking at lender-specific ITIN programs and comparing their written terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is an ITIN mortgage?

It is a home loan for a borrower who uses an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number instead of a Social Security number. Some fit Fannie Mae's rules when the borrower is lawfully present; most are lender-specific programs.

Can I get a mortgage with an ITIN number?

Yes, in some cases. Fannie Mae accepts an ITIN in place of an SSN if the borrower also meets its lawful residency requirement. Otherwise you would need a lender that offers its own ITIN program.

How much does an ITIN loan cost?

There is no single price. Rates, points, fees and prepayment terms vary by lender and by your credit, down payment and documents. Compare written Loan Estimates from more than one lender.

Can I get an FHA loan with an ITIN?

Not under current rules. FHA requires the borrower to be a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident.

Does an ITIN change my immigration status or let me work?

No. The IRS issues ITINs for federal tax purposes only. An ITIN does not authorize work or provide immigration status.
